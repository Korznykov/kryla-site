Who we are

KRYLA Com­mu­nica­tions is a full-service inter­na­tional video pro­duc­tion company with offices in Vienna and Kyiv.

We operate at the inter­sec­tion of video, com­mu­nica­tion and strategy, helping busi­nesses, inter­na­tional organ­isa­tions and plat­forms com­mu­nic­ate their message effect­ively — from short-form content to docu­ment­ary films.

We deliver pro­jects across Europe and work with clients of all scales — from local busi­nesses and per­sonal brands to inter­na­tional cor­pora­tions and devel­op­ment pro­grammes.

For us, video is not just content — it is a tool that influ­ences:

  • brand trust
  • public per­cep­tion
  • busi­ness com­mu­nica­tion
  • decision-making

Our core

Impact is the key cri­terion in every project.

We use video as a stra­tegic tool:

  • to build trust

  • to strengthen brands and plat­forms

What we do

  • Inter­na­tional & Insti­tu­tional Pro­jects

    Video for inter­na­tional pro­grammes, founda­tions and global ini­ti­at­ives. We work with pro­jects that shape cities and coun­tries, drive public dia­logue and com­mu­nic­ate trans­forma­tion.

  • Busi­ness & Eco­nomic Plat­forms

    Video as a tool for busi­ness com­mu­nica­tion and posi­tion­ing. We work with com­pan­ies of all sizes — from local busi­nesses to inter­na­tional cor­pora­tions. We help explain complex products, strengthen brands and build trust.

  • Global Events

    Video for events where clarity and posi­tion­ing matter. We work with inter­na­tional forums, summits and global-scale events. Our goal is to ensure you are not only seen, but clearly under­stood.

  • Per­sonal & Human Stories

    Video for people whose voice creates impact — experts, entre­pren­eurs, thought leaders and public figures.
